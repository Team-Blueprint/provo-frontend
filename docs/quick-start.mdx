## Trigger Paga events with the Demo Simulator

Open the floating **Demo Simulator** drawer in the bottom-right corner of the app to simulate real-world webhook and edge-case events:

1. **Simulate Paga Bank Transfer:** Fires a signed `POST /webhooks/paga` event to transition an `AWAITING_FUNDING` job to `FUNDED`.
2. **Simulate Duplicate Callback:** Replays an existing `paga_event_id` to demonstrate idempotent deduplication (returns `200 OK` without double-crediting the ledger).
3. **Simulate Invalid Hash:** Sends a tampered signature to verify rejection and logging in the [Admin Audit View](/admin).
4. **USSD Simulator (`/ussd`):** Walk through the `*347*900#` feature-phone session for workers without smartphones.
