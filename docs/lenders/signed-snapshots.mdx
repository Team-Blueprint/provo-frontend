Every lender report and completed job receipt can be independently verified without trusting a screenshot.

## Receipt verification

When the final day of a job reaches `PAID`, the job transitions to `COMPLETED` and Provo issues a permanent receipt (`receipt_number`, e.g. `PW-2026-00412`). Anyone with a receipt number can verify its authenticity and settlement status via `GET /jobs/verify/{receipt_number}`.
