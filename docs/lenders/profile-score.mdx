---
title: "Profile score (0–100)"
description: "How Provo calculates the 0–100 Profile Score, applies anti-gaming safeguards, and assigns tier bands."
---

The scoring engine (`app/domain/scoring.py`) is a deterministic function over a worker's verified job and day history. Every point is explained in the report's `reasons` array and summarized as a **0–100 Profile Score** on both the Worker Dashboard and Lender Report.

## Score components

| Component | Rule | Max points |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Base verification** | Awarded once at least one confirmed or auto-released day exists | **15 pts** |
| **Work days quality** | `+2.0 pts` per `CONFIRMED` / `PAID` day (`×0.7` if employer is new to system); `+1.0 pt` per `AUTO_RELEASED` day; `0 pts` for `DISPUTED` | **35 pts** |
| **Unique verified payers** | `+8.0 pts` per distinct employer | **24 pts** |
| **Repeat employers** | `+12.0 pts` per employer with 2 or more separate jobs | **24 pts** |
| **Active earning months** | `+5.0 pts` per calendar month (`YYYY-MM`) with confirmed work | **15 pts** |

<Note>
**Anti-gaming safeguards:**
- **Single-payer concentration cap:** No single employer can contribute more than **35%** of total work-day points.
- **Circular-flow penalty:** Transfers cycling between the same accounts incur a **-20 pt** penalty and surface a concentration notice on the report.
- **Cash-job cap:** Non-escrow cash days are capped at **20%** of total milestone points.
</Note>

Total score is clamped to **0–100**.

## Tiers & score ranges

| Score range | Dashboard tier | Backend band | What it means |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `85 – 100` (`80 – 100` API) | **Established** | `TIER_1_EXCELLENT` | Strong multi-month record across repeat employers; eligible for best lender terms |
| `65 – 84` (`60 – 79` API) | **Trusted** | `TIER_2_SOLID` | Steady record with active employer confirmations; eligible for tool & working-capital credit |
| `40 – 64` (`40 – 59` API) | **Emerging** | `TIER_3_DEVELOPING` | Growing record; starter micro-credit eligibility while building repeat clients |
| `0 – 39` | **Building** | `TIER_4_UNVERIFIED` | Early or disputed record; needs additional funded and confirmed jobs |
