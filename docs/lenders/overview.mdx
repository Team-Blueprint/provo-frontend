Traditional underwriting models rely on salary slips or six months of bank statements. For the 83% of African workers in the informal economy, bank deposits fail to answer the most basic underwriting questions:

- Was this transfer payment for real work, or a personal gift from a relative?
- How many distinct clients actually pay this artisan?
- How consistently do they work across months?

Provo solves this with a structured, consent-gated **Lender Report** (`/report/{token}` or `GET /reports/{token}`).

## Key underwriting guarantees

1. **Money locked before work:** Every scored day is backed by upfront Paga escrow funding, rather than self-reported cash.
2. **Silence is discounted:** Days that auto-released after 24 hours count for half as much as actively confirmed days, and reports flag when `auto_release_ratio > 50%`.
3. **Tokenized client diversity:** Payers appear as deterministic tokens (`Client #A4C1`), letting your model verify repeat clients and unique payers without exposing personal PII.
4. **Explicit fraud flags:** Circular flows, shared phone/Telegram identities, and velocity bursts are surfaced directly on the report.
