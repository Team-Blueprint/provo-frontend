Traditional loan checks rely on salary slips or six months of bank statements. For most skilled workers in Africa, bank deposits leave lenders guessing:

- Was this transfer payment for real work, or a gift from a relative?
- How many different employers actually pay this worker?
- How steadily do they work from month to month?

Provo answers these questions with a worker-approved **Lender Work Report** (`/report/{token}` or `GET /reports/{token}`), combining a deterministic **0–100 Profile Score** and tier band with the underlying verified signals so lenders can apply their own underwriting rules.

## Built-in lender safeguards

1. **Money locked before work:** Every recorded day is backed by an upfront Paga transfer, never self-reported cash.
2. **Active confirmation vs. 24h auto-release:** Reports separate days actively confirmed by an employer or supervisor from days that auto-released after 24 hours of silence.
3. **Masked employer IDs:** Employers appear as consistent IDs (`Employer #A4C1`) so you can verify repeat business and client diversity without exposing personal phone numbers.
4. **Clear fraud flags:** Circular transfers, shared phone or Telegram accounts, and sudden job bursts appear directly on the report.
