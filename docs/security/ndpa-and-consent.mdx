title: "NDPA & consent posture"
description: "Data minimization, explicit worker consent, employer notices, and payer tokenization."
---

Provo is designed around the **Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA)** and **CBN Consumer Protection Framework** principles of purpose limitation, data minimization, and informed consent.

## Worker consent

- Lenders cannot query a worker's full history directly.
- Every report link (`/report/{token}`) requires an explicit `Consent` row created by the worker (`POST /workers/{id}/shares`) with a named recipient and expiry timestamp.
- Workers can list active grants (`GET /workers/{id}/shares`) and revoke consent at any time (`POST /workers/{id}/shares/{token}/revoke`), immediately invalidating the report token (`410 Gone`).

## Employer privacy & tokenization

- **Employer notice:** When funding a job, employers are informed that confirmed work outcomes contribute to the artisan's verified income record.
- **Payer tokenization:** On lender reports, employer names and phone numbers are replaced with deterministic `payer_tokens` (e.g., `Employer #4B9E`). Lenders can verify employer diversity and repeat business without seeing personal identities.
- **Dispute exception:** Full employer identities are accessible only to internal admins resolving an active dispute.
