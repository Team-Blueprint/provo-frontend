title: "Consent & sharing reports"
description: "How workers generate expiring lender links and revoke access at any time."
---

Your work history belongs to you. No lender can view your Provo work report without an active, worker-authorized share token.

## Generating a lender link

1. Open [Consent & Share](/worker/consent) in your worker workspace.
2. Enter the lender's name (for example, `LAPO Microfinance` or `FairMoney`) and choose an expiry window (such as 7 or 30 days).
3. Review the exact fields included in the report:
   - Total settled earnings, active months, and confirmation breakdown (`Employer confirmed`, `Supervisor confirmed`, `Auto-released`)
   - Verified days, repeat employers, and active months
   - Tokenized employer IDs (for example, `Employer #8F2A`), without employer names or phone numbers
4. Confirm consent (`POST /workers/{id}/shares`) to receive a unique URL (`/report/{token}`).

## Managing & revoking access

Every share link records `granted_at`, `expires_at`, and `revoked_at`. Workers can list active and past share grants via `GET /workers/{id}/shares` and revoke any token immediately via `POST /workers/{id}/shares/{token}/revoke`, which causes `GET /reports/{token}` to return `410 Gone` (`CONSENT_EXPIRED_OR_REVOKED`).

<Tip>
Under Nigeria's Data Protection Act (NDPA) and CBN consumer protection rules, consent must be specific, informed, and revocable. Every consent grant and revocation is written to the append-only audit log.
</Tip>
