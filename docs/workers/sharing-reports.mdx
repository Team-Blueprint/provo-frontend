Your work history belongs to you. No lender can view your Provo credibility report without an active, worker-authorized share token.

## Generating a lender link

1. Open [Consent & Share](/worker/consent) in your worker workspace.
2. Enter the lender's name (for example, `LAPO Microfinance` or `FairMoney`) and choose an expiry window (such as 7 or 30 days).
3. Review the exact fields included in the report:
   - Your 0–100 credibility score, tier band, and score breakdown
   - Verified days, repeat clients, and active months
   - Tokenized client IDs (for example, `Client #8F2A`), without client names or phone numbers
4. Confirm consent (`POST /workers/{id}/shares`) to receive a unique URL (`/report/{token}`).

## Revoking access

Every share link records `granted_at`, `expires_at`, and `revoked_at`. Revoking a link immediately causes `GET /reports/{token}` to return `410 Gone` (`CONSENT_EXPIRED_OR_REVOKED`), cutting off further access.

<Tip>
Under Nigeria's Data Protection Act (NDPA) and CBN consumer protection rules, consent must be specific, informed, and revocable. Every consent grant and revocation is written to the append-only audit log.
</Tip>
