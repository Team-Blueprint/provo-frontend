title: "Sign up & payouts"

---

Artisans can join Provo through three entry points, all backed by the same identity record and phone number.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Telegram Bot">
    1. Open an employer's SMS invite link (`t.me/...`) or search for the Provo bot.
    2. Tap **Share Contact** so Telegram provides your verified Nigerian phone number (`+234`).
    3. Pick your primary trade from the fixed list (`BRICKLAYING`, `PAINTING`, `PLUMBING`, `CARPENTRY`, `ELECTRICAL`, `WELDING`, `TAILORING`, `MECHANIC`) and enter your Paga account number.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Web Workspace">
    1. Visit `/sign-in?role=worker` and switch to **Create account**.
    2. Enter your full name, Nigerian phone number, primary trade, and a 6-digit numeric PIN (`POST /auth/signup`).
    3. Verify with the 6-digit SMS one-time passcode (`POST /auth/verify-otp`). Returning workers sign in directly with phone + 6-digit PIN (`POST /auth/login`).
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="USSD (*123#)">
    1. Dial `*123#` in the USSD simulator (`/ussd`).
    2. Check your balance (`GET /ussd/balance`), view pending jobs (`GET /ussd/pending-jobs`), report today's work done (`POST /ussd/report-done`), or withdraw to your Paga payout account (`POST /ussd/withdraw`).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Paga payout account & shareable payment link

Workers manage their Paga payout account number, alert channel preference, and availability in [Worker Settings](/worker/settings) (`GET` / `PATCH /workers/{id}/settings`), and can copy their persistent payment link from `GET /workers/{id}/payment-link` to send directly to employers.

## Public artisan directory

Available workers appear in `GET /workers/available` with only their name, trade, area, verified paid day count, and badge tier:

| Verified paid days | Directory badge |
| :--- | :--- |
| `0` days | `UNVERIFIED` |
| `1–9` days | `BRONZE_VERIFIED` |
| `10–29` days | `SILVER_VERIFIED` |
| `30+` days | `GOLD_VERIFIED` |

Personal phone numbers and payout accounts are never exposed in the public directory.
