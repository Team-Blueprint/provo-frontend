Provo is built for skilled informal workers who work for named employers and are paid per job or per day.

## Who fits best

<Columns cols={3}>
  <Card title="Building trades" icon="hammer">
    Bricklayers, painters, plumbers, electricians, carpenters, and welders working multi-day site contracts.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Tailors & stylists" icon="scissors">
    Fashion makers and hairdressers with repeat employers saving toward sewing machines or generators.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Auto mechanics" icon="wrench">
    Independent repairers paid per job by returning vehicle owners, building credit for diagnostic tools.
  </Card>
</Columns>

<Info>
Walk-in street traders and commercial drivers receive dozens of small anonymous payments per day, so a job-by-job funded workflow is not designed for them in v0.1.
</Info>

## Why workers use Provo

- **Money is locked before Day 1:** You see `FUNDED` status on your job before picking up your tools. No more chasing an employer after finishing work.
- **Daily payouts:** Each confirmed day pays out directly to your Paga account so you do not have to wait until the end of a multi-week site job.
- **24-hour protection:** If an employer forgets to tap Confirm after you report a day done, the scheduler auto-releases your pay after 24 hours.
- **You own your record:** Lenders can only view your verified work report when you explicitly generate a consent-gated share link.
