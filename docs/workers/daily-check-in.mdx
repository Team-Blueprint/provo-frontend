---
title: "Daily check-in & 24h window"
description: "How workers mark each day done and how payouts release."
---

Once an employer funds a job (`job.status = FUNDED`), each day (`1..N`) starts in `PENDING`.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Mark the day done">
    At the end of the workday, tap **Mark Day Done** in the Worker workspace (`/worker/jobs`), reply **Done** in Telegram, or select the active job in `*123#`.

    This calls `POST /jobs/{job_id}/days/{day_number}/done`, moving the day to `DONE_REPORTED` and setting `release_at = done_at + 24 hours`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Employer or supervisor reviews">
    The employer and any assigned site supervisor receive a notification. A single tap on **Confirm** moves the day to `CONFIRMED`, records an active confirmation on your work history, and enqueues an immediate outbox payout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Automatic 24h fallback">
    If neither the employer nor the supervisor disputes within 24 hours, the background scheduler picks up the row with `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, transitions it to `AUTO_RELEASED`, and disburses the day's pay to the worker's Paga account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
You can only mark a day done when the job is `FUNDED` or `IN_PROGRESS`. If the employer has not finished funding the one-time NUBAN, the UI clearly shows `AWAITING_FUNDING` so you never start unbacked work.
</Note>
