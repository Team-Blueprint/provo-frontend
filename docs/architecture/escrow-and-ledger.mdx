---
title: "Money ledger & guaranteed payouts"
description: "How Provo tracks every kobo and makes sure confirmed days never lose a payout."
---

Provo stores every amount in whole kobo and records every movement in a permanent ledger (`FUNDED`, `PARTIAL_FUNDED`, `EXCESS_HELD`, `RELEASED`, `REFUNDED`).

## The balance rule

For every job at any moment:

```text
Money paid out + Money refunded <= Money received
```

When a job is completely finished (`COMPLETED` or `CANCELLED` with all refunds settled):

```text
Money paid out + Money refunded == Money received
```

## Guaranteed payout queue (no lost payments)

When an employer or supervisor confirms a day, or when the 24-hour timer auto-releases a day, three things happen together inside **one database transaction**:

1. The day's status changes to `CONFIRMED` or `AUTO_RELEASED`.
2. A `RELEASED` entry is written to the ledger.
3. A `PENDING` payout row is added to the payout queue with a unique reference key.

Because all three save together, a server crash can never confirm a day without queuing its payout. A background worker sends each queued payout to Paga using its unique reference key (so retries never pay twice) and marks the day `PAID` only after Paga confirms the transfer.
