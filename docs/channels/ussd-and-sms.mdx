Not every bricklayer or market tailor carries an active smartphone data plan on site. Provo supports feature-phone and SMS fallback flows alongside Telegram and Web.

## USSD (`*347*900#`)

The interactive USSD simulator at [`/ussd`](/ussd) demonstrates the menu flow for artisans on basic phones:

1. Dial `*347*900#` to open the Provo session menu.
2. Register with trade and Paga payout account, view funded jobs, or mark today's work as `DONE_REPORTED`.
3. Receive SMS confirmation when the client or supervisor confirms and Paga disburses the daily tranche.

<Info>
Because production telco USSD shortcodes require aggregator provisioning, the USSD channel is simulated in the demo environment and clearly labelled as simulated.
</Info>

## SMS invitations

When a client invites an artisan by phone number before the artisan has started the Telegram bot, Provo dispatches an SMS containing a `t.me` deep link with an invitation token. Pressing **Start** in Telegram automatically links the waiting funded job to the worker's profile.
