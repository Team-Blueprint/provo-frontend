Not every bricklayer or market tailor carries an active smartphone data plan on site. Provo supports feature-phone and SMS fallback flows alongside Telegram and Web.

## USSD (`*123#`)

The interactive USSD simulator at [`/ussd`](/ussd) is backed by live backend endpoints (`/ussd/*`) and demonstrates the menu flow for artisans on basic phones:

1. Dial `*123#` to open the Provo session menu (`POST /ussd`).
2. Check wallet balance (`GET /ussd/balance`), view pending funded jobs (`GET /ussd/pending-jobs`), report today's work done (`POST /ussd/report-done`), or withdraw settled earnings (`POST /ussd/withdraw`).
3. Receive SMS confirmation when the employer or supervisor confirms and Paga disburses the daily tranche.

<Info>
Because production telco USSD shortcodes require aggregator provisioning, the USSD channel is simulated in the demo environment and clearly labelled as simulated.
</Info>

## SMS invitations

When an employer invites an artisan by phone number before the artisan has started the Telegram bot, Provo dispatches an SMS containing a `t.me` deep link with an invitation token. Pressing **Start** in Telegram automatically links the waiting funded job to the worker's profile.
