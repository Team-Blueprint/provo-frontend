---
title: "The one rule"
description: "Why self-reported income counts for nothing, and how Provo labels every work day."
---

A worker's own entry never creates a verified work record.

A record in Provo requires two external facts:
1. **Locked employer money** settled into a job's one-time Paga virtual NUBAN before work starts.
2. **A recorded decision** on each work day by the employer or a designated site supervisor.

If a worker types a job into an app on their own, it is a draft and is never included in lender reports.

## How daily outcomes are labelled

When a worker marks a day as done (`DONE_REPORTED`), a 24-hour confirmation window begins. Every settled day carries a clear outcome label so lenders can distinguish active sign-offs from automatic releases:

| Day status | How it happens | Report label | Payout behaviour |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `CONFIRMED` / `PAID` | Employer or named supervisor taps **Confirm** | `Employer confirmed` or `Supervisor confirmed` | Immediate payout to worker's Paga account |
| `AUTO_RELEASED` | 24 hours pass with no dispute | `Auto-released (24h)` | Automatic scheduler payout; tracked separately on reports |
| `DISPUTED` | Employer or supervisor disputes within 24h | `Disputed` | Day freezes; held for admin resolution |
| Unfunded / Draft | No locked Paga payment behind the job | Not shown on lender reports | Worker cannot mark days done |

<Warning>
Silence is not confirmation. Auto-released days still pay the worker after 24 hours so a forgetful employer cannot withhold earned wages, and lender reports explicitly separate `Auto-released (24h)` days from actively confirmed days.
</Warning>

## Why we dropped cash self-logging

Early prototypes allowed workers to log cash jobs and ask employers to confirm retroactively. In practice, retro-confirmations are easy to fake between friends and lack proof that money changed hands.

By locking the full job total upfront via Paga Collect and disbursing daily tranches only after work is reported, every naira on a Provo report is backed by a settled NIBSS NIP or Paga Checkout transaction.
