---
title: "The one rule"
description: "Why self-reported income counts for nothing, and how Provo weights every work day."
---

A worker's own entry never creates a credit record.

A record in Provo requires two external facts:
1. **Locked client money** settled into a job's one-time Paga virtual NUBAN before work starts.
2. **A recorded decision** on each work day by the client or a designated site supervisor.

If a worker types a job into an app on their own, it is a draft and carries zero weight in the credibility score.

## Evidence outcomes and weights

Not all released days carry the same trust signal. When a worker marks a day as done (`DONE_REPORTED`), a 24-hour confirmation window begins.

| Day status | How it happens | Score weight | Payout behaviour |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `CONFIRMED` / `PAID` | Client or named supervisor taps **Confirm** | **Full weight (2.0 pts/day)** | Immediate outbox payout to worker's Paga account |
| `AUTO_RELEASED` | 24 hours pass with no dispute | **Reduced weight (1.0 pt/day)** | Automatic scheduler payout; flagged if >50% of history |
| `DISPUTED` | Client or supervisor disputes within 24h | **Zero weight (0 pts)** | Day freezes; held for admin resolution |
| Unfunded / Draft | No locked Paga payment behind the job | **Zero weight (0 pts)** | Worker cannot mark days done |

<Warning>
Silence is not confirmation. Auto-released days still pay the worker after 24 hours so a forgetful client cannot withhold earned wages, but the scoring engine discounts them so lenders see whether clients actively confirmed the work.
</Warning>

## Why we dropped cash self-logging

Early prototypes allowed workers to log cash jobs and ask clients to confirm retroactively. In practice, retro-confirmations are easy to fake between friends and lack proof that money changed hands.

By locking the full job total upfront via Paga Collect and disbursing daily tranches only after work is reported, every naira on a Provo report is backed by a settled NIBSS NIP or Paga Checkout transaction.
