## Why employers trust locked job pay

- **One-day maximum exposure:** Even on a 10-day contract, pay releases one day at a time as work is reported and confirmed.
- **No ghost workers:** Funds are only disbursed for days actually worked and confirmed (or auto-released after 24h without a dispute).
- **Any Nigerian bank works:** Fund jobs in 15 seconds via normal NIBSS NIP transfer from OPay, UBA, Access, GTBank, or Kuda with no bank mandate or BVN authorization required.
- **Optional site supervisor:** Busy contractors can assign a foreman on site to confirm or dispute daily work at `/supervisor`.
