Channels & Auth

  • Web app, Telegram bot, and USSD (*123#) share one backend.

Money & Payouts

  • One-time 10-digit Paga NUBAN per job.
  • 24-hour auto-release timer and retryable payout queue.

Trust & Lending

  • Verified work signals (confirmed days, active months, repeat employers).
  • Consent-gated lender reports with masked employer IDs.
